📖 Free Sample — Chapter 1: The Singularity Descends

The deep autumn wind of the Qinling Mountains carried a scent like ground rust as it squeezed forcibly through the fissures of Ghost Sorrow Canyon, stinging the skin like a blade. Lin Shen wiped a sheen of sweat from his forehead. The salty liquid ran down his brow bone and into his eyes, stinging with a fiery burn.

Squinting against the damnable afterglow of the setting sun, he adjusted the heavy 3D laser scanner set up before him. “Lin, are you done gathering yet?” Captain Zhang’s gritty voice crackled over the walkie-talkie, mixed with the static hiss of current, sounding like someone chewing on gravel. “It’s getting dark. That place is cursed; even the local hunters don’t dare stay there overnight.

Pull out, now.” “Last set of point cloud data. Five minutes,” Lin Shen pressed the talk button and replied. As a contractor for the Provincial Surveying and Mapping Institute, Lin Shen’s job was monotonous and dry: carrying equipment weighing over fifty pounds, climbing every fold of the Qinling Mountains like a goat, all to fill in the final few blank spots for the government’s “Digital Qinling” project. The place he stood now had no name on the map; the coordinates simply displayed “Unnamed Valley.” The vegetation here was tinted an uncomfortable shade of deep purple. It was quiet—too quiet. There was not even the chirping of insects, only the whistling sound of the wind passing through dead tree branches.
