# 双通道光学网络的 Heisenberg 极限参数估计协议

> 大白话比喻：好比你有两根光纤，想知道信号在里面怎么走、相位怎么变。传统办法一根根测，测不准还互相干扰；这个方案用纠缠光束同时测4个参数，精度达到物理极限——像同时用4把纳米尺子量同一块晶格，每把尺子都不干扰别的。

## Phenomenon

A fully Gaussian and experimentally feasible scheme for the simultaneous estimation of the four real parameters that characterize a two-channel optical network. The scheme utilizes a two-mode squeezed probe and balanced homodyne detection at both output ports, achieving Heisenberg scaling (1/N precision scaling instead of the standard quantum limit 1/√N).

The quantum Fisher information matrix (QFIM) bound is saturated for all four parameters simultaneously — representing a complete multiparameter estimation protocol at the ultimate quantum limit. The four parameters are the two transmission amplitudes and two phase shifts of a 2×2 complex transmission matrix.

## The "Wasteland" Twist

If the Arena's underlying rendering engine has optical-like signal channels (which is plausible in a Reality-as-Code architecture where information propagates through waveguide-like conduits), this protocol becomes a **simultaneous multiparameter probe of the render engine's internal state**.

**Weaponization vectors:**

1. **Sandbox Parameter Extraction** — The two-mode squeezed vacuum probe acts as an entangling sensor that can read out 4 render parameters at the quantum limit. This enables characterization of the sandbox's internal channel matrix without classical noise accumulation.

2. **Coherent State Tomography** — The protocol's ability to simultaneously estimate all four parameters means it can construct a complete phase-space portrait of any two-channel subsystem in a single shot measurement. Deployed against the Arena walls, this could map the boundary conditions of the simulation.

3. **Heisenberg Advantage** — 1/N scaling means that with N=10⁶ photons, precision improves by factor 10³ over classical schemes. With N=10¹² (feasible with pulsed squeezed lasers), precision reaches 10⁻⁶ radian — enough to detect nanometer-scale distortions in spacetime fabric.
