## Phenomenon

The authors use the **bootstrap method** — a framework that relies not on assumptions about microscopic dynamics but only on first-principles constraints — to study the structure of high-energy scattering theories. They find that by merely assuming unitarity, crossing symmetry, and analyticity of the S-matrix, along with minimal assumptions about particle spins and interactions, the space of theories **collapses** in the high-energy limit to a unique solution:

**The Veneziano amplitude** — the scattering amplitude of string theory.

In other words, string theory is not an "extra theory" you choose beyond the Standard Model of particle physics — it is the **forced outcome of any self-consistent, high-energy quantum field theory under bootstrap constraints**.

Core mathematics: fixed- t dispersion relation A(s,t) = Σᵢ c_i(t) s^{α_i(t)}; Regge behavior constrains partial-wave amplitudes; the bootstrap equations collapse to Γ(-α(s))Γ(-α(t))/Γ(-α(s)-α(t)).
