$$H_{a\gamma} = g_{a\gamma} \int d^3x \, a(x) \, \mathbf{E}(x) \cdot \mathbf{B}(x)$$

where $g_{a\gamma} \approx \alpha/\pi f_a$ is the axion-photon coupling constant. In free space, this conversion is inefficient. The key innovation: **engineered semiconductor quantum well heterostructures** where the bandgap is tuned to match the axion mass $m_a$. When $m_a$ matches the subband energy gap, the conversion is **resonantly enhanced**.

The semiconductor lattice provides the necessary momentum kick (via the inverse Primakoff mechanism), allowing the virtual photon to go on-shell. The expected signal power is $P \sim 10^{-24}\,\text{W}$ — barely above the quantum noise floor, detectable only with superconducting bolometry at $T=0.1\,\text{K}$.
