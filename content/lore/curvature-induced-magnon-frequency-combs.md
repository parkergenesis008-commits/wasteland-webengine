## Plain-language Analogy

> *This is like striking a flat drumhead and hearing only a single tone. But if you curve the drumhead, a single strike produces an entire row of evenly spaced notes—each interval between notes perfectly equal. Now replace this with spin waves in a magnet: the curved magnetic film acts like that curved drumhead, the microwave is the drumstick, and what emerges is a "magnon frequency comb"—a full set of frequency teeth, each spaced exactly equally.*

> *A more forceful analogy: you have a straight wire that can only vibrate at one frequency. Twist the wire into a spring shape, and suddenly you generate N harmonic resonances. This PRL is essentially saying—bend the magnetic film, and spin waves can produce precision equally spaced multi-frequency output like a laser frequency comb.*

## Phenomenon

Small curvature in magnetic thin films, under microwave driving, can generate frequency combs of spin waves (spin wave / magnon)—i.e., a series of magnon modes with strictly equally spaced frequencies. This effect is forbidden in flat magnetic films: curvature induces an effective Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction (DMI) term, whose coupling strength is proportional to the curvature tensor κ², thereby activating the three-magnon confluence process (three-magnon confluence) that is otherwise prohibited by selection rules.

Core physical mechanisms:
1. **Curvature-Induced DMI**: The geometric curvature tensor κ_ij of the bent magnetic film produces an effective DMI field, breaking inversion symmetry
2. **Three-Magnon Parametric Process**: Curvature activates three-magnon confluence (ω₁ + ω₂ → ω₃), generating equally spaced frequency sidebands
3. **Frequency Comb Spacing**: Δω = ω₀/n, where ω₀ is the driving frequency, tunable via the curvature radius
4. **Dispersion Engineering**: Curvature gradients can locally control the magnon group velocity v_g and effective mass m*
