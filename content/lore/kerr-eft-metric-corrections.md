## Phenomenon

Higher-derivative corrections to the Kerr metric parameterized by EFT coefficients $c_i$ (dimension-6+ operators) are computed numerically across all spin parameters $a$. The corrections modify the multipole moment structure of rotating black holes:

$$g_{\mu\nu} = g_{\mu\nu}^{\text{Kerr}} + \sum_i c_i \, \mathcal{O}_i^{(n)}$$

where $\mathcal{O}_i^{(n)}$ are higher-derivative operators and $c_i$ are EFT coefficients. The corrected multipole moments $\delta M_l$, $\delta J_l$ vary with spin $a$, producing observable shifts in ringdown gravitational wave signatures.

Key point: **The corrections persist across the entire spin spectrum** — they do not appear only at extreme or slow rotation, but leave a systematic footprint throughout the full range $0 < a < 1$.
