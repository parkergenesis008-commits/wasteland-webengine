Through a **postselected Closed Timelike Curve (CTC)**—i.e., a noisy postselected closed timelike curve—quantum information can be transmitted from a **future sender** to a **past receiver**. Unlike traditional quantum channels, the channel mapping $N_{A\to B}(\rho)$ here exploits the postselection operation $P = |0\rangle\langle 0| \otimes I$, creating a **time-reversing channel** within the quantum circuit.
