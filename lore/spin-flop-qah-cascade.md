## 1. The Physics Challenge and Breakthrough (The Physics)
In conventional quantum computing, external magnetic fields—especially "in-plane magnetic fields" parallel to the two-dimensional plane—are typically fatal sources of interference that destroy quantum states.
While studying an intrinsic antiferromagnetic topological insulator (7 septuple layers of MnBi2Te4, capped with an AlOx protective layer), the Tsinghua team discovered a highly counterintuitive "magical" phenomenon:
*   **Spin Flips and Flops:** When applying gate voltages and perpendicular/in-plane magnetic fields, the internal antiferromagnetic spin configuration undergoes remarkably rich rotations and flips.
*   **In-Plane Field Enhancement Effect:** A breakthrough finding—applying an "in-plane magnetic field" did not destroy the system; instead, it **enhanced** the quantum anomalous Hall effect, triggering **"cascaded quantum phase transitions."**
