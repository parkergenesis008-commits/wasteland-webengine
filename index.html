About the book

Alien Dimensions: The Shepherd's Wasteland is a novella-length hard science fiction story — about 16,000 words, four parts in one volume — in which Lin Shen, a contractor mapping a nameless valley, discovers that the emptiness he is measuring is not empty. What follows is first contact, a dark-forest bargain, and a debt that comes due thirty thousand years later.

It is written for readers of The Three-Body Problem who want the physics taken seriously: the companion encyclopedia below documents the real research behind each device, and labels the rest as invention.

About the Author

Miancheng Yu (余免成) is the author of Alien Dimensions: The Shepherd's Wasteland (Chinese title 《异维空间：牧羊人的荒原》, planned) — a hard science fiction novel where a geological surveyor discovers a rift in spacetime and is pulled into a war between civilizations that treat reality as source code.

This encyclopedia is the companion to the novel: every entry translates a real condensed-matter physics principle (topological metamaterials, Einstein-Cartan torsion, Kagome-lattice engineering) into speculative technology, and each page separates established research from the author's extrapolation and from pure setting.
