FAQ

What is Alien Dimensions: The Shepherd's Wasteland about?

Surveyor Lin Shen discovers an absolute void in reality while mapping China's Qinling Mountains, is abducted to the dark side of the Moon, and learns that Earth is a "cosmic pasture" for high-dimensional entities. Bonded to an alien artifact and made a "Warden", he fights the predatory Void Race across a story spanning 30,000 years.

Is it like The Three-Body Problem?

Yes in spirit: a Chinese protagonist, dark-forest cosmic sociology, first contact and hard physics. It is much shorter and adds a tragic romance and timeline manipulation.

Is the science real?

The speculative technology is built on real condensed-matter physics (topological metamaterials, Einstein–Cartan torsion, Kagome lattices, KPZ universality). The companion encyclopedia cites the arXiv papers behind each idea.

What is the KBC Void and how does the novel use it?

The KBC Void is a real, unusually low-density region of the universe around the Milky Way. The novel places that same void at the centre of the story, as something that shields the galaxy, at a cost.

How long is it, and when was it released?

It is novella-length: about 16,000 words (roughly 42 pages in the author's PDF), divided into four parts — not four separate books. It was first released on Apple Books on 2026-03-13; other stores list their own dates.
