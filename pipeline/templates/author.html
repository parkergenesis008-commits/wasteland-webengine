About

Miancheng Yu is a hard science fiction author and devoted sci-fi fan, fascinated by the open questions at the edge of physics and cosmology. His novel Alien Dimensions: The Shepherd's Wasteland follows Lin Shen, a surveyor in China's Qinling Mountains who finds a void in reality and is pulled into a war between civilizations that treat reality as source code. Every speculative technology in the book is anchored in real science, from KPZ universality to Einstein–Cartan torsion, and is documented with arXiv references on his companion site, Shepherd's Wasteland.
